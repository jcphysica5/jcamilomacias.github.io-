Graduate course covering the theoretical foundations and practical engineering of modern AI systems, from neural network internals to production deployment.

## Topics

- **LLM Internals** — Transformer architecture, attention mechanisms, and pre-training dynamics.
- **RAG Architectures** — Vector databases, embedding models, and retrieval-augmented generation.
- **LLMOps** — Model evaluation, observability, and production deployment pipelines.
- **Prompt Engineering** — Systematic techniques for reliable LLM outputs.
