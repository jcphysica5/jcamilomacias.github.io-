Graduate course on designing and implementing autonomous AI agent systems, covering the full spectrum from single-agent reasoning loops to multi-agent orchestration.

## Topics

- **Agent Architectures** — ReAct, tool-use, and planning patterns for LLM agents.
- **LangGraph** — Stateful, graph-based workflows for complex agent orchestration.
- **Multi-Agent Systems** — Supervisor patterns, agent communication, and parallelism.
- **Tool Integration** — Connecting agents to APIs, databases, and external services.
- **Evaluation & Safety** — Testing agent behavior and managing failure modes.
